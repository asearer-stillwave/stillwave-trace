Radio roles

Trace runs on one dongle, or on two: SDR 1 keeps the aircraft map on full time (1090 MHz) while SDR 2 plays airband or Spectrum. Pick a job for each dongle once; it is saved on this box by serial number.

Looking…

Tip: put the SDR 1 and SDR 2 stickers from the kit on the dongles. Identify: tap it, then unplug a dongle; the card that goes grey is that one.

Older setup: write a label into the dongle

1.0.9 method, still supported: plug in only the dongle you want to label, pick its job, then unplug and replug it. Roles saved above win over labels.

Over SSH

sudo sdr-dongles list · sudo sdr-dongles assign SERIAL=adsb SERIAL=radio · sudo sdr-dongles swap · sudo sdr-mode status
