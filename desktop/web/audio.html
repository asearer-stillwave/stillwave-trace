Output

HDMI plays through the monitor's speakers. Headphone jack is the Pi's 3.5 mm socket. Changing this restarts the audio stream for a second.

Where FM plays

Phone stream: listen on the Listen page on your phone. Pi speaker: the box plays it on the Bluetooth speaker (or its own audio jack/HDMI when no speaker is connected). Changing this restarts the FM stream for a second.
