Receiver position

…

Used for distances, range rings and “Center on me”, and handed to the decoder. Stored on this box in /etc/sdr/adsb.env. Tip: a rounded position (2 decimals) is plenty.

Change the gain with − / + / Auto on the map (saved as ADSB_GAIN in /etc/sdr/adsb.env). A 1090 MHz antenna (about 6.9 cm whip) works far better than the FM antenna.
