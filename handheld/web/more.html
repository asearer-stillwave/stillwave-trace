Stillwave Trace · station software

// RESET SDR USB

Power-cycle the dongle's USB port? The current mode stops for about 5 seconds, then starts again.

// TURN SDR OFF

Stops Listen, Map, Scan and Spectrum and releases the dongle, so you can swap the antenna safely. Turn it back on here to resume.

// TURN WI‑FI OFF

Phones on this Wi‑Fi (or on the Trace hotspot) lose the connection right away. The hotspot will not come back on its own. Turn it on again here on the kiosk screen, or reboot.
