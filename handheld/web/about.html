Stillwave Trace —. Everything below works with no internet.

Aircraft models

Looking…

Aircraft data: Mictronics aircraft database, licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By 1.0), via github.com/wiedehopf/tar1090-db. Maps an aircraft's ICAO address to its registration and model. Models can be missing or out of date; the map then shows "Unknown type".

Needs internet (home Wi‑Fi), about 8 MB. Nothing is sent about you or the aircraft you see.

Station bank

Looking…

FM stations: U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC) licensing data. Airband and airports: U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) NASR 28-day subscription, including runway headings. ZIP code positions: U.S. Census Bureau. U.S. government works, public domain. Receive only, not for navigation. Frequencies change; the FAA publishes a new cycle every 28 days.

Needs internet and a content source (More › Download maps), about 2 MB. Checked against the Stillwave signature.

Radio frequencies

Built-in LAX airband presets: FAA Chart Supplement data (via AOPA and airnav.com), checked October 2026. Other airports come from the station bank above. Receive only, not for navigation.
